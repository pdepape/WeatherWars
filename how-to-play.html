THE ONE-MINUTE FORECAST

How to play
Weather Wars.

Your goalFinish turn 10 with more credits than the rival weather desk.
EVERY TURN

Follow three broadcast segments.

The large instruction bar always tells you what to click next.

1
REGION DESK

Claim one open region

Click one pulsing tile marked OPEN. Blue means yours; red means the rival’s.

Good choice: claim beside matching terrain for a future adjacency bonus.
2
BUSINESS DESK

Choose a building, then a tile

First click a building card at the bottom. Then click one of your empty blue regions.

Best matches: Farm + Grassland · Mine + Mountain · Wind + Water · Solar + Desert.
3
WEATHER DESK

Choose weather, then a target

Helpful forecasts glow on your blue buildings. Severe forecasts glow on red rival buildings.

You may play more than one card if you can afford it—or end the turn and save credits.
